# GymPulse Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 31/08/2026_

GymPulse ("the app", "we", "us") is a workout-tracking app operated by GymPulse. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. In short: **your data stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of it.**

## The data the app stores

The app stores the workout information you enter, entirely on your device:

- Workouts, exercises, sets, reps, and weights
- Workout dates, durations, and notes
- Your in-app preferences (units, theme, notification and haptic settings)

This data is saved in a local database on your device. It is **not** sent to us or to any server, and we have no ability to see it.

## We do not collect anything

- We do **not** operate any servers that receive your data.
- We do **not** use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- We do **not** collect crash reports or usage telemetry.
- We do **not** create accounts or require you to sign in.
- We do **not** sell, share, or disclose any data, because we never receive any.

## Data you choose to export

The app lets you export your data to a file. When you use the export feature, the file is handed to your device's standard share sheet, and **you** choose where it goes (for example, Files, email, or another app). Anything that happens to that file after you export it is governed by the destination you pick and its own privacy terms — not by us. Similarly, when you import a file, it is read only from the location you select.

## Notifications

If you enable the rest-timer notification, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated on-device and are not sent through any external service.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** (optional): used only to alert you when a rest timer finishes. You can decline or revoke this at any time in your device settings.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Data retention and deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you are in full control. To delete your data, use the app's delete features or uninstall the app, which removes its local database.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and include the revised policy in an app update.
